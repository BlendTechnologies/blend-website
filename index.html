Frequently asked questions

About Blend's personalization engine

Short answers to what product brands ask before getting started.

What is a personalized product quiz?

A fully customizable quiz on your store that learns each visitor's goals, health, lifestyle, and diet, then matches them to the right products in your catalog through a deterministic scoring algorithm. Not a decision tree, not persona-based. The algorithm is the differentiator.

Which verticals does Blend support today?

Supplements and over-the-counter health is the live vertical, that's where the scoring engine has the deepest content layer (nutrient gaps, RDIs, ingredient interactions, dosing). Beauty and food are on the roadmap. The scoring architecture is portable across categories; the category-specific content layer is what we build out per vertical. If you're not in supplements yet, talk to us about timing.

How does the engine actually personalize?

It scores every product in your catalog against the customer using more than 30 product attributes (ingredients, dosing, interactions, evidence, contraindications, synergies) and user signals spanning goals, sub-goals, conditions, lifestyle, and nutrient gaps inferred from a food-frequency questionnaire. Sport is read as type, intensity and weekly frequency, not just whether you exercise. Each pick comes with the specific reasons that produced it, not just an answer-to-tag match. See the methodology →

What can a customer actually tell you in the quiz?

Goals first, then the signals underneath each one. A customer picks the goals that matter to them, spanning energy, sleep, gut health, hormonal balance, sports performance, healthy ageing and more. Each goal opens a follow-up that captures the specific signals behind it, so “gut health” becomes bloating, irregular habits, discomfort after dairy, and so on. That detail is what the scoring engine reads, alongside diet, lifestyle and biology.

How is Blend different from other quiz tools?

General quiz tools match products to multiple-choice answers using tags or point accumulators. Blend runs deterministic scoring across every product in your catalog, factoring in ingredient interactions, upper-limit safety, synergies, and nutrient gaps inferred from a food-frequency questionnaire. Same input always produces the same output, auditable, defensible, and shippable to brands in regulated categories. Every recommendation carries the reasons that produced it.

How do I get my catalog into Blend?

Upload your catalog through our webapp. Shopify is supported with automatic sync, plus CSV and manual upload for any other source today. WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and other platforms are coming. Our team can help if your catalog has anything unusual.

How does Blend handle safety, interactions, contraindications, ingredient limits?

The engine includes a safety layer that runs on every recommendation: supplement–supplement interaction checks, medication conflict screens, upper-limit gating against tolerable intakes, and synergy pairing. Every flagged interaction or limit is traceable to a source rule, important if you operate in a regulated category.

Is the quiz white-label?

Completely. Your brand, your colors, your domain. No “Powered by Blend” anywhere, as if you had a team of devs build it for you. See the white-label options →

What does Blend cost?

15-day free trial on every plan. Starter from $49/month, Pro from $299/month, Enterprise from $599/month. Plans scale by quiz responses and catalog size. See pricing →

What languages does the quiz support?

English today. Multi-language support, French and other major languages, is coming soon. The quiz text (questions, labels, CTAs) is fully customizable per brand, so when new languages land you can run a localized quiz per market.

Where can I run Blend? Which countries?

Blend is fully GDPR-compliant under UK/EU regulations and the major US state privacy laws (CCPA, etc.). Brands can run quizzes globally, the platform serves customers in any country. Data residency requirements (EU, US, UK) are covered; talk to us during onboarding if you have specific compliance needs.

What analytics do I get back as a brand?

Pro and Enterprise plans include four analytics surfaces:

  • User Analytics, who's taking your quiz: demographics, goals, conditions reported, diet, and more.
  • Quiz Analytics, completion rates, drop-off points, common answer paths, conversion, and more.
  • Product Analytics, which products get recommended most, basket composition, signals on AOV and conversion.
  • Market Analytics (coming soon), category-level trends across your customer base: what your audience cares about, where the gaps are.

Every view is brand-scoped. Cross-brand aggregates are anonymized and never expose individual customer data.

Can I migrate from another quiz tool?

Yes. Most brands switch over in days, not weeks: we map your existing quiz logic into Blend's scoring engine, ingest your catalog, and you keep your existing embed slot. Your old persona/tag rules become inputs to the algorithm.

Can I tune the algorithm to my brand?

Yes. You can adjust scoring weights, exclude product attributes, prioritize specific goal-to-product mappings, and configure brand-specific overrides during onboarding. The deterministic core stays, your tuning is layered on top.
