Supplement brands

Help customers navigate your supplement range with intelligent recommendations tailored to their goals, lifestyle and nutritional needs. They stop guessing in front of a forty-product grid, and the safety checks take out a product that is wrong for them before it can be recommended.

See the supplement use case
Sports nutrition

From muscle growth to endurance and recovery, recommend products tailored to each customer’s training goals and activity levels. The engine carries separate multipliers for weight training and endurance, so someone lifting four times a week and someone training for a marathon get different targets and different plans from one catalogue. You write no rules for either, and you don’t need a catalogue of your own to start.

Clinics & practitioners

Support product selection with transparent recommendations, suitability checks and clear explanations. Medication conflicts, contraindications and upper limits are checked on every recommendation, so what a client leaves with is a product recommendation with its reasoning attached, not a diagnosis and not a substitute for your own judgement. Work from your own dispensary, or from our product list.
