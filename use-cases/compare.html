Why brands switch

Beyond the feature checklist.

What the table doesn't show: the outcomes that move brands from a tag-based quiz to Blend.

01

Fully customizable scoring

Most quiz tools lock you into persona templates and decision-tree branches that look tunable but constrain real personalization. Blend lets you adjust scoring weights per attribute, exclude product fields you don't want considered, prioritize goal-to-product mappings, and configure brand-specific overrides — on top of a deterministic core that stays predictable.

02

Trust converts

Customers who understand why a product was picked convert at higher rates than customers shown a black-box "you got this." Blend ships every recommendation with the specific reasons that produced it — nutrient gaps closed, goals matched, safety checks passed. Visible reasoning builds the trust that closes the sale.

03

Defensible for regulated categories

Supplements with structure-function claims, OTC products, and regulated markets all share the same requirement: when a customer, a lawyer, or a regulator asks how a recommendation was made, you can show the math. Deterministic scoring + traceable signals + source-rule references = a defensible recommendation, not a marketing pitch.

Frequently asked questions

Buyer questions.

How is Blend different from other quiz tools?

General quiz tools match products to multiple-choice answers using tags or point accumulators. Blend runs deterministic scoring across every product in your catalog, factoring in ingredient interactions, upper-limit safety, synergies, and nutrient gaps inferred from a food-frequency questionnaire. Same input always produces the same output — auditable, defensible, and shippable in regulated categories. Every recommendation carries the reasons that produced it.

Can I migrate from another quiz tool?

Yes. Most brands switch over in days, not weeks. We map your existing quiz logic into Blend's scoring engine, ingest your catalog, and you keep your existing embed slot.

What integrations does Blend support?

Native Shopify catalog sync, CSV upload, and manual upload for any other source today. WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and other platforms are on the roadmap.
