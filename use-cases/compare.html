Beyond the feature checklist.

What the table doesn't show: what changes when every pick is scored and explained.

01

Your priorities, on a core that stays predictable

General quiz tools offer flexible branching and weighted scoring for any category. Blend adds a nutrition layer on top. You give each product a priority and choose the goals your business offers, and brand-specific overrides are configured with our team during onboarding, all on a deterministic core that stays predictable.

02

Give customers a reason to trust every recommendation

A customer who can see why a product was picked can judge it for themselves, instead of being asked to take a black-box "you got this" on faith. Blend ships every recommendation with the specific reasons that produced it, nutrient gaps closed, goals matched, safety checks passed.

03

Traceable for regulated categories

Supplements with structure-function claims, OTC products and regulated markets share one requirement: when somebody asks how a recommendation was made, you can show the working. Every recommendation is traceable to the customer and product information used to generate it, down to the source rule behind each safety check.

Buyer questions.

How is Blend different from other quiz tools?

General quiz tools match products to multiple-choice answers using tags or point accumulators. Blend runs deterministic scoring across every product in your catalogue, factoring in ingredient interactions, upper-limit safety and the nutrient gaps estimated from a food-frequency questionnaire. Same input always produces the same output, and every recommendation is traceable to the information that produced it.

Can I migrate from another quiz tool?

Yes. Blend can help you migrate your catalogue and configure your personalised quiz. Setup time depends on your existing system and catalogue complexity. Your old persona and tag rules become inputs to the engine.

What integrations does Blend support?

Shopify connects directly and syncs daily; a CSV upload or adding products by hand covers any other source, and you can add products from Blend's own catalogue. A listing in the Shopify App Store is in review, and WooCommerce, BigCommerce and other platforms follow.
