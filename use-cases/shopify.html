From your store to every customer's plan

Match, recommend, launch: three of the six steps, on a Shopify catalogue.

01 · MatchEvery product, read for what is in it.

Blend AI reads each product your store sends, including the marketing names and the products made of several ingredients, and matches it to the engine. Nothing is saved until you have reviewed it.

  • Single nutrients and multi-ingredient formulas alike
  • The goals each product serves and who it is not for, filled in from the engine
  • You approve every match before it reaches a customer

How Match works

02 · RecommendA wellness plan, not just a basket.

Every customer gets a plan of their own: your products ranked for them, the reason under each pick, and what their diet leaves short. You choose how it looks and what it shows.

  • Picks scored for one customer, each with its reason
  • Macros and nutrient gaps against their own targets
  • Product cards that view the product, add it to the cart, or add it to a bundle
  • Your layout, colours and words

See a sample report

03 · LaunchOn your store, in one line.

Embed the quiz on a page, open it from a button as a pop-up, or share a direct link. Lock it to your own domains so nobody else can run your quiz.

  • Works on any Shopify page or theme section
  • Changes stay a draft until you publish
  • Your branding, and on Pro and above no Blend badge

How launching works

Shopify-specific questions

Does Blend integrate natively with Shopify?
Yes. Connect your store by its address and Blend reads your published products. Turn on Daily sync and new and changed products come across every day. A listing in the Shopify App Store is in review.
Is the embed Shopify-theme-friendly?
Yes. The quiz goes on any Shopify page, landing page, product page or blog post, opens from a button as a pop-up, or runs from a direct link. It wears your colours and fonts throughout, and on Pro and above it carries no Blend badge.
Can I migrate from another quiz app?
Yes. Blend can help you migrate your catalogue and configure your personalised quiz. Setup time depends on your existing system and catalogue complexity. Your old persona and tag rules become inputs to the engine. See the comparison.
How long does it take to launch?
Live in an afternoon: connect your store, match your catalogue, make the quiz yours and embed it.
