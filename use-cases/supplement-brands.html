Shoppers already look for this by name: six separate search terms, among them “custom vitamins”, each run around 5,000 searches a month.

Those people want a product built around one person. A static catalogue answers with a bestseller list. A quiz answers with a plan.

A real personalisation engine, not a decision tree.

Supplement logic built in: reference intakes, ingredient interactions, upper limits, and the goals behind every recommendation.

25 product attributes scored

Category, form, serving, timing, the goals each product serves and who it is not suitable for: 25 attributes recorded for every product, each read when it is scored, not just a tag.

17 safety gates on every pick

Supplement interactions, medication conflicts, upper-limit gating against tolerable intakes, and contraindications. Every flag traces to a source rule.

Personal targets, not a generic chart

Reference intakes from your market's own authority, adjusted for ethnicity, sun exposure, activity level and lifestyle, per customer and per nutrient. Every pick answers the customer's own target.

Safety layer on every pick

Interaction checks, medication conflicts and upper-limit gating against tolerable intakes run before a product can be recommended. Every flagged limit traces to a source rule.

Every recommendation, traceable to the rules that produced it.

The engine is deterministic: the same answers always produce the same picks, and every pick carries the reason that produced it. That matters most where someone asks why: supplements with structure-function claims, OTC products and regulated markets.

Engine questions.

How does the supplement quiz personalise recommendations?

It scores every product in your catalogue against the customer using 25 product attributes and a full profile of user signals. Each pick comes with the specific reasons that produced it.

Is the quiz engine auditable?

Yes. The engine is fully deterministic, same input always produces the same output. Every recommendation carries the reasons that produced it. Important if you operate in a regulated category.

What safety checks run on each recommendation?

Supplement interaction checks, medication conflict screens and upper-limit gating against tolerable intakes: 17 checks that can remove a product, none of which can be switched off. Every flagged interaction or limit is traceable to a source rule.

How is this different from a tag-based persona quiz?

Most quiz tools match products to answers with tags or point totals. Blend scores every product in your catalogue for each customer with one engine, applies the safety checks, and returns the reason behind each pick. See the full comparison →
