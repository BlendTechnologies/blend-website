Tag-based quizzes don't actually personalize.

Most Shopify quiz apps work the same way: a decision tree mapped to product tags. Customer picks "muscle gain" — they see your protein. Customer picks "sleep" — they see your magnesium. That's segmentation, not personalization.

Blend computes a numeric, deterministic score for every product in your catalog per customer, factoring in goal matches, sub-goal signals, ingredient interactions, upper-limit safety, synergies, and inferred nutrient gaps. Same answers always produce the same recommendations. Every pick comes back with the reasons that produced it.

If you operate in a regulated category, that auditability isn't nice-to-have — it's how you defend your recommendations to customers, lawyers, and regulators.
