Frequently asked questions

Buyer questions.

How is Blend different from Octane AI, Rebuy, or Lifery?

Those tools match products to multiple-choice answers using persona tags or decision trees. Blend computes a numeric, deterministic score per product per customer, factoring in ingredient interactions, upper-limit safety, synergies, and inferred nutrient gaps. Same input always produces the same output. Every recommendation carries reasons.

What integrations does Blend support?

Native Shopify catalog sync, CSV upload, and manual upload for any other source today. WooCommerce, BigCommerce, and other platforms are on the roadmap. Our team can help if your catalog has anything unusual.

Can I migrate from another quiz tool?

Yes. Most brands switch over in days, not weeks. We map your existing quiz logic into Blend's scoring engine, ingest your catalog, and you keep your existing embed slot.
