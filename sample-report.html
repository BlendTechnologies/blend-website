    What Alex told the quiz.

    Forty-three answers: who Alex is, what Alex wants help with, and a typical day of eating. Alex is vegetarian, sleeps five to six hours, and reported trouble falling asleep and afternoon dips.

  2. 02Picks

    Eight picks, each with its reason.

    Three are Essential: the nutrients Alex's diet leaves furthest short. Five are Recommended for what Alex told the quiz. Melatonin would have matched Alex's sleep answers; it is prescription-only in the UK, so the engine left it out.

  3. 03Macros

    A day, estimated.

    Intake is estimated from Alex's answers and set against Alex's own targets. Free sugars and saturated fat run over their limits, and omega-3 is at 8% of target, which is why it heads the plan.

  4. 04Gaps

    Only what needs attention.

    Vitamin D3 at 6% of target and iron at 57%, with the foods that supply them. Everything else is on track, so it stays off the page.

  5. 05Safety

    Safe before it is shown.

    17 checks can remove a product before it reaches a plan, and none can be switched off. Here, Vitamin D3 carries a caution on its card, and the plan closes with the disclaimer and a way to ask for a human review.
