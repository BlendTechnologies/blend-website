What Blend does

Build your quiz and embed it on your site. Every customer who finishes it gets a plan like this one — their own needs matched to your catalogue, in your own colours.

  • Every pick carries its reason. A figure from that customer's own answers, not “others also bought”.
  • 25 product attributes scored against their intake, their goals and the gaps between the two.
  • 17 safety gates run before anything is recommended: pregnancy, medications, upper limits, diet.
  • Live in an afternoon. One embed code, and your catalogue as a spreadsheet.
  • A quiz editor. Questions, branding and which sections show, changed from your dashboard rather than by pasting code.
  • Analytics. Completion, drop-off by question, click-through, and which products your customers actually ask for.
