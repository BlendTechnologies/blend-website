01Connect

Start with your catalogue.

Connect Shopify, import a CSV, pick from our catalogue or add products by hand. Your catalogue is the starting point for every recommendation.

Why it’s different

Connect your catalogue with a click and it re-syncs on a schedule, so price and stock changes flow through on their own. Or upload a CSV and let us work out what goes where.

02Match

Connect products to the data.

Blend reads what’s in each product, matches it to its research database, and fills in the data the engine scores. You review every match before anything is saved.

Why it’s different

Blend AI reads your whole catalogue and pre-fills everything the engine needs from what is actually in each product: every ingredient, including the ones combined into a single formula. You review it, change whatever you want, and nothing is saved until you apply it.

How the engine works →

03Design

Your questions. Your look and feel.

Build the experience in your brand’s voice. Edit the content, adjust the design, and preview the quiz as your customers will see it.

Why it’s different

Start from your industry’s template and make it yours in the editor. The only limit on how it looks and feels should be what you can picture. If you picture something the editor cannot do yet, our team will build it. If you would rather not start from a blank page, let Blend AI draft it.

04Launch

Put it where customers shop.

Embed the quiz on a page, open it from a button as a pop-up, or share a direct link.

Why it’s different

Paste one script once, into your site, your app or an email campaign. Everything you change afterwards updates itself, with no second visit to the code. Lock it to the domains you choose, so nobody else can run your quiz.

05Recommend

Their answers. A plan of their own.

The engine scores every product in your catalogue against each customer’s answers, targets and your rules, then explains every pick.

Why it’s different

This goes well beyond tags and personas. Every product runs through a multi-layer engine that weighs the customer’s answers, the product’s own data and the safety rules. Every pick comes back with the reason behind it, and the method underneath that.

See the full sample report →

06Analyse

Learn from every journey.

Hear your customers in their own words. See where they engage, where they drop off, and what they need from your range.

Why it’s different

This is first-party data you own outright: what lifts your quiz’s conversion, who your customers actually are, what they came for, and what they expect a personalised range to include. Catalogue gaps turns the last of those into a stocking list: the products your customers keep asking for and you do not yet sell.
