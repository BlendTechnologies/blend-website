The quiz button goes wherever you want

Direct customers to your product page, add to their cart straight away, let them build a bundle, or turn the products section off completely. You choose how it works, and you can change it any time without touching the embed code.

Three button modes, or no button at all

Pick the experience that fits the type of store you run. Settings are controlled from your dashboard, so changing how the quiz behaves never requires pasting new code.

View product

Sends the customer to your product page wherever it lives, on your store, on a third party marketplace, or anywhere else. They see the full details and add to cart themselves.

Add to cart

Adds the product to the customer's cart without leaving the results page, so your store's cart count updates immediately and they can keep browsing or check out.

Add to bundle

Lets the customer collect multiple recommended products on the page, then add the whole set to their cart in one go. Useful when results typically show more than one product.

Or disable the products section entirely if you only want to collect customer signals and show recommendations elsewhere, outside the quiz. The section carries an enabled flag you can toggle from your dashboard.

One script tag, works everywhere

1

Paste one line of code

Copy the embed script from your dashboard and drop it wherever you want the quiz to appear. Works in Shopify, Squarespace, Wix, WordPress, and anything else that accepts custom code.

2

Change settings without re-pasting

Switch button modes, adjust branding, edit questions, or turn sections on and off from your dashboard. The embed stays the same. Settings resolve server side each time the page loads, so updates are instant.

3

Display inline or as a popup

The quiz fills the width and height of wherever you place it. Show it as a page section, or attach it to a button that opens a modal. Both work with the same embed code.

4

Connect your store and link your catalogue

Connect your Shopify store in seconds and Blend imports your products automatically. Each product is matched to the recommendation engine's ingredient model, and from then on the quiz recommends from your own range. Brands without Shopify can add products manually or upload a CSV. Product clicks and cart adds are tracked back to the quiz session.
