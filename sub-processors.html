Sub-Processors

Last updated: 5 September 2026  ·  Blend Technologies LTD (United Kingdom)

This page lists the third-party services (sub-processors) that Blend Technologies engages to help deliver the platform. Each is bound by a written data processing agreement. We give clients advance notice of any change to this list.

Processor Purpose Data categories Region Transfer mechanism DPF-certified?
Vercel Webapp hosting (frontend, serverless functions, quiz builder, merchant dashboard, auth) Merchant account data; end-user quiz submissions EU (fra1 Frankfurt) N/A (EU processing region) N/A
Supabase Database + auth; stores end-user quiz data End-user quiz submissions (health data, identifiers, consent); merchant account data EU (eu-west-1 Ireland) N/A (EU processor) N/A
Render — personalization FastAPI personalization engine hosting End-user quiz submissions; pseudonymised logs (90-day retention) EU (Frankfurt) N/A (EU processor) N/A
Render — checkout Billing service hosting Merchant billing data only EU (Frankfurt) N/A (EU) N/A
Stripe Payment processing (merchant billing, not end-customer) Merchant billing data US / Ireland EU-US DPF Yes — EU-US DPF, UK Extension, Swiss-US DPF
Airtable CRM for business contacts and sales prospects (NOT end-user quiz data) Business contact data US UK IDTA / EU SCCs 2021/914 No — relies on SCCs + UK IDTA
Resend Transactional email (auth, password resets, quiz results) Email addresses, recipient names, email content US UK IDTA / EU SCCs 2021/914 Yes per Resend DPA (verify on gov registry)
Shopify Source of merchant catalogue and order data (analytics only, no customer PII) Product catalogue, order IDs, timestamps, cart events Multi-region Per Shopify DPA Per Shopify

EU-US Data Privacy Framework certifications must be verified annually at dataprivacyframework.gov. This list is reviewed quarterly.

Bubble.io was retired as an active sub-processor on 5 September 2026 and processes no Blend data.
