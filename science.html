Safety

17 checks that can remove a product. None can be switched off.

gate functions in the engine’s safety chain. Sixteen remove a product and one adds a warning without removing anything.

Diagnosed condition conflict

Removes products that the product record says to avoid with a condition the customer has told us about.

In Isabel’s run
Pregnancy and breastfeeding safety

Removes products that are not safe in pregnancy or while breastfeeding.

Prenatal audience only

Removes products meant only for people trying to conceive, pregnant or breastfeeding from everybody else’s plan.

Sex-specific products

Removes products formulated for one sex from the other sex’s plan.

Beta-carotene for smokers

Removes products that raise risk for current smokers, beta-carotene above all.

In Isabel’s run
Age band suitability

Removes products with no established requirement or dose for the customer’s age and sex.

In Isabel’s run
Iron need check

Removes standalone iron from plans for people whose physiological need for it is low, because unneeded iron is not benign.

Low body weight safety

Removes products that are unsafe or inappropriate at a very low body weight, and signposts support instead.

Training-only products

Removes sport and training products from the plan of somebody who reports no structured exercise.

In Isabel’s run
Total caffeine ceiling

Removes caffeine-bearing products once the total caffeine across the whole plan would pass the daily ceiling.

No reference intake ceiling

Removes single-nutrient products for nutrients with no established reference intake, where no real shortfall can be shown.

Tolerable upper limit

Removes any product whose serving would take the customer’s total intake of a nutrient past its tolerable upper limit.

In Isabel’s run
Hard medication conflict

Removes products that cannot be taken alongside a medication the customer says they are on.

In Isabel’s run
Declared medication caution

Adds a warning to products whose own label cautions against a medication the customer is on, and removes nothing.

Warns, removes nothing
Diet compatibility

Removes products that clash with the customer’s diet, in both directions: incompatible ingredients, and vegan alternatives offered to somebody who is not vegan.

In Isabel’s run
Market ingredient legality

Removes products containing an ingredient that cannot legally be sold as a food supplement in the customer’s market.

Stimulants against sleep

Removes caffeine-bearing products when the customer’s goal is better sleep, or when they already sleep six hours or fewer.

In Isabel’s run

Plus 6 plan checks

redundancy and diet-credit checks, which are not safety checks. They shape a sensible plan rather than a safe one, and are not safety checks.

  • One multivitamin only — Removes every multivitamin after the best-matching one.
  • Multivitamin against standalone — Removes a single-nutrient product when a recommended multivitamin already supplies that nutrient.
  • One per product family — Removes products from a family where taking more than one together adds nothing, keeping the best match.
  • Duplicate active ingredient — Removes a product whose active ingredient is already covered by another product in the plan.
  • Redundant complex — Removes a combination product whose contents are already covered by the single-nutrient products in the plan.
  • Already met by diet — Removes a product for a nutrient the customer’s own food intake already supplies at or above their target.
Markets

Targets follow the country you sell in.

Reference targets, upper limits and which ingredients are allowed depend on the market. Blend applies the right authority for each country.
