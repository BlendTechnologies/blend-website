Frequently asked questions

Platform questions.

What is a personalized product quiz?

A fully customizable quiz on your store that learns each visitor's goals, lifestyle, and context, then matches them to the right products in your catalog through a deterministic scoring algorithm. Not a decision tree, not persona-based. The algorithm is the differentiator.

Which verticals does Blend support today?

Supplements and over-the-counter health is the live vertical. Beauty and food are on the roadmap. The scoring architecture is portable across categories; the category-specific content layer is what we build out per vertical.

Can I use Blend for a non-supplement catalog?

The scoring architecture is portable across product categories. The supplement-specific content layer (RDIs, ingredient interactions, dosing logic) doesn't apply to other verticals — but the quiz UX, scoring framework, catalog ingestion, and white-label embed do. Talk to us if you're not in supplements yet.

How long does it take to launch?

Most brands go live in under 24 hours. Upload your catalog, customize the quiz from a template, embed. See the full FAQ →
